Case study: CardioBot

A clinical decision support assistant for suspected acute coronary syndrome. It answers from published guidelines instead of model memory, and it sends each kind of question to the model best suited for it.

83%reduction in pre-arrival decision support time
895passages from the 2023 ESC ACS Guidelines

What it does

A clinician asks a question, uploads an EKG, or requests a drug calculation. CardioBot picks the right model for the job and answers from the guideline text.

How it is built

Guideline passages are stored in ChromaDB for retrieval. Claude Sonnet handles clinical questions, Gemini Vision reads EKG images, and GPT-4 handles drug calculations. LangGraph coordinates the workflow.
